Genesis

The Studio Story

Founded in 2009 by Principal Architect Nathan Van Rensselaer (NVR), our practice took shape from a fundamental rebellion against fleeting trends and cold, impersonal modernism.

Educated between Zurich and Florence, Van Rensselaer envisioned a studio where the rigor of European planar architecture would harmoniously commune with the natural warmth of unlacquered bronze, patinated lime wash, and hand-chiseled travertine.

Foundational Truths

Core Philosophy Pillars

The unchanging ethos steering every commission, sketch, and joint.

PILLAR 01 architecture

Intentional Proportion

Form follows serenity and function. We compose architectural volumes with deliberate negative space, framing daylight so rooms breathe with sacred, uninterrupted quiet.

PILLAR 02 nature

Tactile Honesty

We reject imitation. Every surface features raw porous travertine, natural heavy linen, aged cast bronze, and FSC-certified smoked woods intended to age with graceful patina.

PILLAR 03 precision_manufacturing

Precision Engineering

Millimeter-level zero-tolerance joinery. Concealed reveals, shadow gaps, and bespoke millwork designed to fit seamlessly into architectural fabric without visible hardware.
